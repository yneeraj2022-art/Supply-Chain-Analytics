# Supply Chain Analytics System

## 1. Project Overview

The **Supply Chain Analytics System** is a data analytics project developed to analyze supply chain operations using Python, Pandas, SQL, Matplotlib, and Flask.

The system analyzes sales, inventory, supplier performance, delivery delays, and monthly sales trends.

The project converts raw supply chain data into meaningful analytical insights and presents the results through an interactive Flask dashboard.

---

## 2. Project Objective

The main objectives of this project are:

* Analyze total sales and orders
* Identify high-performing products
* Analyze category-wise sales
* Analyze monthly sales trends
* Monitor inventory levels
* Identify low-stock products
* Analyze supplier performance
* Calculate supplier on-time delivery rate
* Identify delayed orders
* Analyze delivery delays by supplier
* Analyze delivery delays by month
* Present analytical results through a web dashboard

# 8. Sales Analysis

The sales analysis module performs:

### Total Sales

Calculates the total revenue generated from all orders.

### Total Orders

Calculates the total number of orders.

### Product-wise Sales

Analyzes total sales generated by each product.

### Category-wise Sales

Analyzes sales according to product categories.

### Monthly Sales Trend

Analyzes how sales change month by month.

### Supplier-wise Sales

Analyzes sales generated through different suppliers.

---

# 9. Inventory Analysis

The inventory module analyzes:

* Current stock
* Reorder level
* Low-stock products
* Reorder quantity
* Inventory value
* Product-wise inventory

A product is considered low stock when:

```text
Stock < Reorder Level
```

Inventory value is calculated using:

```text
Inventory Value = Stock × Unit Price
```

---

# 10. Supplier Analysis

Supplier performance is analyzed using:

* Total orders
* Total sales
* Delayed orders
* On-time orders
* Average delivery days
* On-time delivery rate

The on-time delivery rate is calculated as:

```text
On-Time Rate =
(On-Time Orders / Total Orders) × 100
```
